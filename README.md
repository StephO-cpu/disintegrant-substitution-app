
# Disintegrant Substitution Analyzer

A web app made with Streamlit that allows local Ghanaian disintegrants (cassava, rice starch) to be compared against imported disintegrants (crospovidone) in terms of disintegration time, cost savings, and regulatory risk.

# The Problem
Pharmaceutical manufacturing companies in Ghana heavily depend on imported excipients. Volatile foreign exchange rates, high shipping costs, import duties, suck-up capital. Local solutions do exist, but the manufacturers are looking for a faster method to determine whether the alternative will work and be cost effective without going through months of lab testing and trial and error.

# The Solution
This tool is an in silico model to predict disintegration time according to swelling ratios and mechanism of action. It then works out what savings might be possible over a year of use and gives a regulatory risk score (such as flagging that local materials must be qualified with suppliers).


# Limitations & Disclaimer

This is an in silico model that has been based on published literature and standard excipient properties. This is not a substitute for formulation testing in the physical laboratory.
It is designed for education and portfolio use, for the purpose of explaining computational pharmaceutics.
